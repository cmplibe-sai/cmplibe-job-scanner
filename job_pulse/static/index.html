Google Sheets Credentials & Settings

Local Config

When enabled, newly scraped opportunities are immediately appended to your Google Spreadsheet.

Paste the full spreadsheet URL or the ID from the browser address bar.
Make sure to share your Google Sheet with the client_email found in this JSON key with Editor permissions.

Quick Setup Guide

  1. Create / Open a Google Sheet in your Google Drive.
  2. Create a Service Account in Google Cloud Console with the Google Sheets API & Google Drive API enabled.
  3. Download the Keys > JSON file and paste the JSON content into the form on the left.
  4. Share your Google Sheet: Click the Share button on your Google Sheet and add the service account email (e.g. your-service-account@project.iam.gserviceaccount.com) with Editor access.
  5. Click Test Connection and you're ready! All columns, deduplication, and timestamps are handled automatically.
Deduplication Guaranteed: The sync engine checks existing Job IDs before appending, ensuring zero duplicate rows even on repeated scans.
