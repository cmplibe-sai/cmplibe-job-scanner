Quick Setup Guide

  2. Create a Service Account in Google Cloud Console with the Google Sheets API & Google Drive API enabled.
  3. Download the Keys > JSON file and paste the JSON content into the form on the left.

👤 Team Member Quickstart Guide

Welcome to cMPLiBe AIScanner! As a team member, you have direct access to search live job opportunities across 9+ portals, add new target companies to the automated Radar, and review recruiter posts.

1 Searching Opportunities

  • Go to Opportunity Explorer.
  • Search by Role (e.g., Category Manager, React Dev) or by Company.
  • Click 'Scan Portals Now' to scrape LinkedIn, Naukri, Internshala, and Unstop in real-time.
  • Use the Technical vs Non-Technical filters to refine your sourcing list.

2 Adding Target Companies

  • Go to Target Company Radar.
  • Click the green '+ Add Target Company' button.
  • Enter the company name and career page link.
  • Specify keywords (e.g., developer, product, intern).
  • Click Save — the system will now monitor this company automatically!

3 Opportunity Outputs

  • Live Table: Click any job title to open the direct official application page.
  • Google Sheet Sync: All opportunities are automatically categorized and logged in Google Sheets.
  • Recruiter Posts: View direct LinkedIn hiring posts with recruiter contact info.

👑 Administrator Master Playbook

Administrators manage system security, outgoing mail servers (Resend API), Google Sheets service account credentials, and team member provisioning.

Team User Management

In Email & System Settings under Team User Accounts, admins can create new member accounts, assign roles (admin vs member), reset passwords, and toggle account activation.

24/7 Email Alert Dispatch

Use the Resend HTTPS API (Port 443) preset. Resend delivers alerts over HTTPS, completely avoiding cloud SMTP port blocks (Port 465/587). Ensure sender header uses alerts@cmplibe.com.

24/7 Hosting on Hostinger

Hostinger VPS provides 24/7 dedicated compute with persistent storage. Unlike Render Free (which sleeps after 15m), Hostinger never resets the database and runs background alerts continuously.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

How do I add and monitor a new company in Target Radar? Target Radar

To add any company to the automated Radar watchlist:

  1. Open the Target Company Radar tab.
  2. In the Add Target Company form on the left, enter the Company Name (e.g. Amazon, Swiggy, Paytm, Khatabook).
  3. Paste their official Career Page / ATS link (Greenhouse, Lever, Workday, Amazon.jobs, etc.).
  4. Optionally specify role keywords (e.g. developer, product manager, intern) or leave empty for ALL roles.
  5. Click '+ Add to Watchlist' — the system will immediately start monitoring this company 24/7!
How do I export or copy sourced opportunities into my Google Sheet? Export & Sync

You have two instant options in Opportunity Explorer:

  • Copy for Google Sheets: Click the green 📋 Copy for Google Sheets button. Open your Google Sheet, click on any empty cell, and press Ctrl + V (or Cmd + V). All columns (Title, Company, Location, Work Mode, URL, Sourced IST) will paste formatted automatically!
  • Download Excel / CSV: Click 📥 Download Excel / CSV to download a complete spreadsheet file on your computer.
  • Automatic Live Sync: In the Google Sheet Live Sync tab, all newly discovered jobs are automatically logged into your configured cloud Google Spreadsheet.
How do I filter between Technical and Non-Technical roles? Role Filters

In Opportunity Explorer and All-India Job Radar, use the Role Category dropdown:

  • 💻 Technical Only: Filters for Software Engineers, Frontend, Backend, Fullstack, DevOps, Data Science, AI/ML, and QA roles.
  • 👔 Non-Technical Only: Filters for Category Managers, Sales & Business Development, HR & Talent Acquisition, Marketing, Finance, Operations, and Customer Success.
  • All Role Types: Displays all available positions across both domains.
What is the difference between Opportunity Explorer, Target Radar, and All-India Radar? Product Features

1. Opportunity Explorer: On-demand real-time search engine. Enter any keyword, role, location, or company to instantly scrape 9+ job portals and download results.

2. Target Company Radar: Dedicated company watchlist. Scrapes ATS career pages and recruiter feeds for your specific target firms (e.g. Swiggy, Amazon, Khatabook) and sends email alerts whenever new openings appear.

3. All-India Job Radar 🇮🇳: 24/7 automated nationwide discovery engine scanning fresh openings across major tech and business hubs in India.

How to find direct LinkedIn HR & Recruiter hiring posts? Recruiter Sourcing

In Opportunity Explorer, click the 'Recruiter & HR Posts' view tab at the top of the listings table. This displays direct LinkedIn posts made by hiring managers and talent recruiters (with poster names, contact details, and role descriptions) so you can reach out directly!
